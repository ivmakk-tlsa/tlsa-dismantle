# Dismantle recipes and salvage chances

Use **Dispose** in your inventory to break down an unwanted item. Any recovered parts go straight into your inventory. The button still says "Dispose", and the usual quantity or confirmation prompt still applies.

Some items always return something; others can leave you empty-handed. **Disposing still removes the item, even when you get nothing back.** This guide lists the mod's default returns.

## At a glance

| What you dispose of | What you get |
|---|---|
| Canned food or bottled water listed below | Its empty container, guaranteed |
| Bandages | 1 Rag, guaranteed |
| Battery | 50% chance of 1 Electronics |
| Craftable weapon, attachment or crafting part | One randomly chosen recipe ingredient |
| Gun without a crafting recipe | 1 Scrap (90%) or 1 Firearm Parts (10%) |
| Metal melee weapon without a crafting recipe | 1 Scrap (95%) or 1 Melee Parts (5%) |
| Attachment without a crafting recipe | 1 Scrap, guaranteed |
| Molotov Cocktail, Can Bomb, Beeper Bomb or Box Mine | A chance to recover parts; see [Throwables](#throwables) |
| Plain Bat, Board, ammo and other items with no salvage recipe | Nothing |

A weapon with a **repair recipe** uses the recipe-ingredient rule too. That means a repaired gun or blade can give you its damaged version back instead of Scrap.

## Food and water

You keep the same empty container you would get from using the item. Disposing of 3 Canned Beans returns 3 Cans.

| Disposed item | Salvage per item |
|---|---|
| Canned Beans | 1 Can |
| Canned Fish | 1 Can |
| Canned Fruit | 1 Can |
| Canned Meat | 1 Can |
| Dog Food | 1 Can |
| Rusty Canned Food | 1 Can |

| Contaminated Water | 1 Plastic Bottle |

## Bandages and batteries

| Disposed item | Possible salvage | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Bandages | 1 Rag | 100% |
| Battery | 1 Electronics | 50% |

Battery uses this rule even though it has a crafting recipe.

## Throwables

These return only some of their ingredients. You cannot recover the Rag from a Molotov Cocktail or the Can used in a bomb.

| Disposed item | Possible salvage | Chance for each part |
|---|---|---|
| Molotov Cocktail | 1 Alcohol | 75% |
| Can Bomb | 1 Explosives | 50% |
| Beeper Bomb | 1 Explosives and 1 Electronics | 33% each |
| Box Mine | 1 Explosives and 1 Scrap | 33% each |

For a single Beeper Bomb or Box Mine, each part has its own chance: you can get both, either one, or nothing. They return Explosives directly rather than the Can Bomb used to craft them.

Grenade, Stun Grenade, Brick, Bottle, Torch and Chem-Light return nothing.

## Craftable weapons, attachments and parts

You recover **one of the recipe's ingredients**, with an equal chance of either choice in the tables below. For example, an Improvised Shotgun gives you either 1 Can or 1 Firearm Parts. You always get one of them.

This covers items that have a recipe, whether you crafted them yourself or found them. Battery, Bandages and the throwables above use their own rules. Craftable ammo and other medical supplies do not give recipe ingredients back.

## Weapons without a crafting recipe

Each weapon gives one material. The chances below are alternatives, so you do not get both from the same weapon.

| Weapon type | Possible salvage | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Ranged weapon | 1 Scrap | 90% |
| Ranged weapon | 1 Firearm Parts | 10% |
| Metal melee weapon | 1 Scrap | 95% |
| Metal melee weapon | 1 Melee Parts | 5% |

Damaged weapons are included if they have no crafting recipe of their own. A plain **Bat** or **Board** returns nothing, and neither does Unarmed. Their crafted upgrades, such as Spiked Bat and Spiked Board, use the recipe tables above.

## Attachments without a crafting recipe

Attachments without a recipe return **1 Scrap, guaranteed**. This includes looted suppressors, scopes, sights, magazines and ammo mods. The three craftable attachments listed above return a recipe ingredient instead.

## Disposing of a stack

Guaranteed returns scale with the quantity you dispose of: 4 Bandages give 4 Rags, for example.

For chance-based returns, the mod works out the total for the stack and rounds any fraction up or down at random. It does not roll separately for every item:

- 2 Batteries at 50% give 1 Electronics.
- 3 Batteries at 50% give 1 Electronics, with a 50% chance of a second.
- 4 Molotov Cocktails at 75% give 3 Alcohol.

For a stack using the crafting-ingredient rule, one ingredient is chosen for the whole disposal and its recipe quantity is multiplied by the number of items disposed.
